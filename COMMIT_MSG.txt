Keep the preview out of search until launch

Adds a NOINDEX switch at the top of tools/build.py, on by default, which
writes noindex, nofollow onto every page. Lisa's Wix site is still the live
brilliantlysane.org, so an indexable copy of her name and copy on a second
domain would compete with it.

Deliberately not blocking robots.txt as well: a crawler that is disallowed
never reads the meta tag, and the bare URL can still be listed. Letting them
crawl and telling them not to index is the combination that actually works.
