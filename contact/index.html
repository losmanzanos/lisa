What happens next

So the wait isn't strange.

I get back to you, we find twenty minutes, and we talk. You tell me roughly what's going on. I tell you how I work and whether I think I'm the right person for it.

If I'm not, I'll say so on that call and point you somewhere better. That happens sometimes and it isn't a bad outcome.

If I am, we schedule a first session. $175 for fifty five minutes, mostly by video, CO Access Medicaid accepted. The rest of the details are here.

Bring your doubts to the free call. That's a good use of it.
